Shake your phone, let fate pick your next meal

Can't decide where to eat? Give your phone a shake and myShakeEat picks a restaurant near you at random — from the mamak downstairs to the kopitiam a few streets away.

Coming soon to Google Play

📳

Shake to pick

Shake your phone or tap the button — the slot machine spins and reveals today's spot. Not feeling it? Shake again.

📍

Near and a little farther

Finds restaurants, cafes and kopitiams around you with an adjustable search radius. Picks are shuffled across the whole area, so places a bit farther away get their turn too.

🥗

Filters that fit you

Open now, halal, takeaway, vegetarian options, no spicy food, no beef — switch on what matters and the pick respects it.

🗺️

Take me there

See the map, the route from you to the door, and opening hours where available. Happy with it? Open your maps app and go.

📸

Food diary

Check in with a photo and a note after you eat. Look back by month: how many meals, how many places, and what you ate most.

⏰

Meal-time reminders

Gentle nudges at breakfast, lunch and dinner — and the occasional late-night supper reminder for when you're feeling peckish.

🌏

Trilingual, local

华语 / English / Bahasa Melayu, with light and dark themes. Made for Malaysia.

🔒

No account needed

No sign-up. Your history, diary photos and settings stay on your phone.

Restaurant data comes from OpenStreetMap contributors via Geoapify. Opening hours, halal and dietary markers come from that community data and may be incomplete — please verify important details, such as halal status, at the venue.
