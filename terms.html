What the app does

myShakeEat picks a place to eat at random from the restaurants near you. Restaurant data comes from OpenStreetMap contributors, provided through Geoapify and the Overpass API.

Accuracy of information

Opening hours, open/closed status, halal, vegetarian and takeaway markers and similar details come from community map data and may be missing, inaccurate or out of date. The “no spicy food” and “no beef” filters are guesses based on a place's cuisine and name and may miss some places. Always rely on the actual situation at the venue — especially for important information such as halal certification or food allergies, please verify it yourself. The app is not responsible for the accuracy of third-party data.
